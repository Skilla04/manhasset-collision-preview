Privacy policy

Effective November 21, 2026

Information we collect

Information you give us

We collect the information you choose to enter when you send us a message or request an estimate or appointment. Depending on the form, this may include:

  • Contact details: your name, phone number, email address and preferred way to be contacted.
  • Vehicle details: year, make, model, color and vehicle identification number (VIN).
  • Request details: a description of the damage, the service you want, and your preferred appointment date and time.
  • Insurance details: whether you are filing a claim, your insurance company and your claim number.
  • Photos: pictures you upload of your vehicle. Photos may show your license plate, VIN, odometer reading, or people and property in the background. To keep uploads small, your browser resizes photos before sending them, which typically removes embedded location data.
  • Messages: anything else you write to us.

Please do not include sensitive information in our forms, such as Social Security numbers, driver's license numbers, payment card or bank details, or health information. We do not need it to prepare an estimate.

Information collected automatically

This website does not use its own cookies, analytics tools, advertising pixels or similar tracking technologies. Like nearly all websites, the servers that host this site automatically record basic technical information when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, the pages you request and the date and time. Our hosting provider uses this information to deliver and secure the site. Some content on this site is provided by third parties that may also collect information, as described under Third-party services.

How we use your information

We use the information we collect to:

  • respond to your messages and questions;
  • prepare repair estimates and schedule appointments;
  • communicate with you about your request, estimate, appointment or repair;
  • work with your insurance company and adjuster when you ask us to;
  • keep business, tax and regulatory records we are required to maintain;
  • prevent spam, fraud and misuse of this website; and
  • comply with the law and protect our rights and the rights of others.

We do not use your information for automated decision-making, and we do not add you to marketing lists. If we ever want to send you marketing messages, we will ask for your permission first.

Calls, text messages and email

When you submit a form, you agree that we may contact you by phone call, text message or email, using the details you provide, about your request, estimate, appointment or repair. These are service messages, not marketing. Message frequency varies with your request. Message and data rates may apply. You can reply STOP to any text message to stop receiving texts, or tell us at any time how you prefer to be contacted. Agreeing to be contacted is not a condition of buying any service; you can always call us at (516) 365-5400 instead.

When you submit a form, you may also receive an automatic email confirming that we received your request.

How we share your information

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for targeted or cross-context behavioral advertising. We share personal information only in these situations:

  • Service providers that help us operate this website and our business, such as our website host, our email provider and FormSubmit, the service that delivers form submissions to our email. These providers may use your information only to provide services to us. According to FormSubmit's policies, it keeps form submissions for 30 days and does not keep uploaded files. FormSubmit may ask you to complete a Google reCAPTCHA check, which is subject to Google's Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
  • Your insurance company or adjuster, when you ask us to work with them on your claim.
  • Businesses that help complete your repair, such as parts suppliers or a rental car provider, only to the extent needed for your service.
  • Legal and safety reasons: when required by law, subpoena or court order, or when needed to protect the rights, property or safety of Manhasset Collision, our customers or others.
  • Business transfers: if our business is sold or transferred, customer information may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to this policy.

Third-party services on this site

  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces on every page. To deliver them, Google receives your IP address and basic browser information.
  • Google Maps is embedded on our home and contact pages. Google may collect information and set cookies when the map loads or when you interact with it.
  • Links to Google, Facebook, Instagram and our website designer take you to sites we do not control.

These companies handle information under their own privacy policies, including Google's Privacy Policy. We encourage you to review the policies of any site you visit.

Cookies, Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control

We do not set cookies or use analytics or advertising trackers on this website, and we do not track visitors over time or across other websites. Because of that, this site does not respond differently to "Do Not Track" browser signals. We do not sell or share personal information for advertising, so there is nothing to opt out of with a Global Privacy Control signal. You can block or delete third-party cookies, including Google's, in your browser settings.

How long we keep your information

We keep the information you send us only as long as we need it to respond to you, provide our services and meet our legal, insurance, tax and record-keeping obligations. After that, we delete it or keep it in a form that no longer identifies you.

How we protect your information

We use reasonable administrative, technical and physical safeguards to protect personal information, consistent with New York law. Our forms are sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. No website or email system is completely secure, however, so we cannot guarantee absolute security. If a security breach affects your personal information, we will notify you as required by applicable law.

Your choices and rights

You may ask us to:

  • tell you what personal information we have about you and give you a copy;
  • correct information that is wrong;
  • delete your information, subject to records we are legally required to keep; and
  • stop contacting you by text, phone or email.

To make a request, call us at (516) 365-5400 or write to us at the address below. We may need to confirm your identity before acting on a request, and we will respond within 45 days. We honor these requests no matter where you live, and we will not treat you differently for making one. Some states give their residents additional privacy rights; we will honor those rights to the extent they apply to us.

Children's privacy

This website is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please call us at (516) 365-5400 and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will post the new version on this page and change the effective date at the top. If we make significant changes to how we use personal information, we will make that clear on this page.
