About us

Family owned since 1956.

Manhasset Collision is a third-generation, family-owned auto body shop on Long Island's North Shore.

Our story

Three generations. One standard.

Manhasset Collision has been part of the North Shore community since 1956. Three generations later, we're still built on the same idea: do honest work, do it right, and treat every customer like a neighbor, because they are.

Whether you've been in a serious accident or just have a scrape, our team takes care of your car from start to finish. We take the worry out of the repair process and get you back behind the wheel as quickly as we can.

A good body shop should be clean, safe and properly equipped. Ours has state-of-the-art equipment and a trusted staff of experienced technicians who can repair almost any kind of body damage, with honest advice at every step.
